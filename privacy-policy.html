Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Building Bridges Behavior Therapy, LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of the individuals we serve and those who visit our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and any information you voluntarily provide in your message. We do not collect sensitive health information through our website contact form.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry and to communicate with you about our services. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Health Information (HIPAA)

As a healthcare provider, we are subject to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Protected Health Information (PHI) collected in the course of providing services is governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices, not this Privacy Policy. Our website contact form is not intended for the submission of PHI.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use basic analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through our website. However, no method of internet transmission is completely secure.

Contact Us
