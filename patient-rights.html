Your Rights

Patient Rights and Responsibilities

Effective Date: May 2026

At Building Bridges Behavior Therapy, LLC, we are committed to treating every individual we serve with dignity and respect. The following outlines your rights as a client receiving services from our agency.

Your Rights

  • To be treated with dignity, respect, and compassion at all times
  • To receive services without discrimination based on race, color, national origin, disability, age, sex, religion, or any other protected characteristic
  • To receive a clear explanation of your treatment plan, including goals, strategies, and expected outcomes
  • To participate in the development and revision of your treatment plan
  • To give or withhold informed consent for treatment
  • To refuse or discontinue services at any time without penalty
  • To have your health information kept private and confidential in accordance with HIPAA
  • To access your records as permitted by law
  • To file a complaint or grievance without fear of retaliation
  • To be informed of any changes to your services in a timely manner

Your Responsibilities

  • To provide accurate and complete information about your child's health, history, and needs
  • To actively participate in your child's treatment and caregiver training sessions
  • To communicate openly with your clinical team about concerns or changes
  • To provide reasonable notice when canceling or rescheduling appointments
  • To treat all staff with respect

Filing a Complaint

If you have a concern about your care, please contact us directly at lindsay@buildingbridges.care. You may also file a complaint with the Ohio Department of Medicaid or the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights.
